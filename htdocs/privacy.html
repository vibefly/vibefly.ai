Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 2026  ·  Last updated: March 2026

vibefly.ai is operated by motiveFx LLC, based in Naugatuck, Connecticut. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

1. Information We Collect

When you fill out a contact form on vibefly.ai, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone number (if provided)
  • Company name (if provided)
  • Your message

We don't collect payment information directly — payments are processed through Stripe, which has its own privacy policy.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to respond to your inquiry, provide and manage the services you've requested, and communicate with you about your project or account. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Infrastructure and Third-Party Services

Our platform runs on Cloudflare, which handles website hosting, DNS, and security (including Turnstile bot protection). Cloudflare may process traffic data as part of normal operations. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

We use Stripe for payment processing. Stripe collects and handles payment data independently under their own privacy policy.

4. Data Retention

We retain your contact information as long as we have an active business relationship with you. If you'd like us to delete your data, contact us and we'll take care of it promptly.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time. Just reach out and we'll respond within a reasonable timeframe.

6. Cookies

We don't run advertising or tracking cookies. Cloudflare may set security-related cookies as part of bot protection.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. The effective date at the top of this page will reflect the latest revision.

8. Governing Law

This policy is governed by the laws of the State of Connecticut.
